Consider scout for unfamiliar local investigation or researcher for external synthesis only when it outweighs startup and handoff cost. Keep tiny tasks and known-path lookups direct. For a known authoritative URL, fetch directly; search only for missing sources or evidence. Retrieve focused snippets first and expand only for evidence gaps. Request a concise answer with references and uncertainties, not exploration logs.
